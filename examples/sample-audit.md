# Example output (fictional account)

> This is an illustration. "@harbourandhearth" is a made-up coffee shop and every number below is invented to show the format.

## Verdict
- **Leaking layer: Send.** Posts are watched but rarely passed on: 3.1 sends per 1k reached vs 9.8 on the shop's own top pillar.
- **Biggest fix:** move the weekly menu-update images to Stories and give those feed slots to the "behind the bar" reels that already earn sends.
- **Expected range:** +10% to +35% sends per month at the same posting volume, if the reel pillar holds its current rate.

## Signal Audit (last 90 days, 84 posts)

| Format | Posts | Median reach | Median sends /1k | Median saves /1k | Share of reach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels | 31 | 2,400 | 7.2 | 4.0 | 71% |
| Carousels | 18 | 1,100 | 2.9 | 8.6 | 20% |
| Images | 35 | 310 | 0.4 | 0.9 | 9% |

- Top 5 posts = 48% of reach. The account lives on outliers; the typical post reaches 900.
- 22 of 35 images are menu updates posted to the feed.
- 6 reels are re-uploads of earlier reels. Their median reach is 40% of the originals'.
- 9 captions start with hashtags; 4 are empty.
- No link tracking: 1,240 profile visits, 0 measurable orders from Instagram.

## Fix list
**Fix first**
1. Add a tracked order link in the bio (one per channel).
2. Stop re-uploading reels. Recut with a new hook instead.
3. Captions: first line is the hook. No hashtag-first captions.

**Do next**
1. Menu updates to Stories plus one Friday roll-up carousel.
2. Behind-the-bar reels from 2 to 4 a week (the Send pillar).
3. One save-worthy carousel a week ("5 drinks under $6").

**Test**
1. Hook test: "specific number" vs "contradiction" on the same reel idea, 5 pairs, metric: sends per 1k.
