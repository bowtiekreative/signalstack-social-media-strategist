# Content playbook

## Hook patterns

Write 5 to 10 hooks per idea across different patterns, then mark the strongest. A hook must work in the first 2 seconds, on screen, with the sound off.

| Pattern | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Contradiction | "Everyone says X. It's Y." | "Posting more isn't hurting your reach. Posting the same thing is." |
| Specific number | a precise, surprising figure | "Three of my 90 posts brought in half my reach." |
| Named stakes | who loses what | "If you run a small bakery, this caption mistake is costing you regulars." |
| Open loop | start the story mid-action | "The moment the client opened the report, she went quiet." |
| Identity call | speak to one person | "Autistic founders, this one's for you." |
| Before / after | show the gap | "Week 1: 300 views. Week 6: 30,000. One change." |
| Myth kill | name and break a belief | "Hashtags don't save a bad hook." |
| Direct question | a question the viewer must answer in their head | "Would your best customer send this to a friend?" |
| Proof first | lead with the receipt | screenshot or result, then the explanation |

Avoid: slow intros, logos first, "Hey guys", greetings, asking for likes.

## Short-form video script (15 to 60 s)

1. **Hook (0-2 s):** on-screen text plus first spoken line, same claim.
2. **Tension or proof (2-10 s):** why it matters, or the evidence.
3. **Payoff (the middle):** the useful part; one idea, no detours.
4. **Earned send moment:** a line that makes passing it on natural ("If someone you know is stuck on this, they need this.").
5. **Call to action:** one, matched to the goal (see below).

Format notes: captions on screen, cuts every 1 to 3 seconds when talking, original audio or licensed sound, no other app's watermark, full resolution.

## Carousel (6 to 12 slides)

1. Cover: one short claim plus a face or a strong image. Must stop the scroll alone.
2. Setup: why this matters now.
3. to N-2. One idea per slide, and every slide ends with a reason to swipe.
N-1. Summary slide made to be saved (checklist, framework, cheat sheet).
N. Call to action slide.

Large type, few words per slide, clear numbering, consistent design.

## Captions

- First line = the hook or the payoff, written for people. Never internal notes, slide specs or labels.
- Body adds what the visual can't: context, the source, the story.
- One call to action.
- Include the words people would search for (platform search reads captions). Hashtags only when they describe the post; a few relevant ones beat many.
- Cite sources for factual claims.

## Calls to action by goal

| Goal | Ask | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | send | "Send this to the friend who needs to hear it." |
| Authority | save | "Save this for your next launch." |
| Community | real question | "What's the one thing you'd add?" (not "comment YES") |
| Followers | follow for a series | "Part 2 tomorrow. Follow so you don't miss it." |
| Leads / sales | profile or link | "The full checklist is in my bio." |
| Support | direct, honest ask | "If this helped, you can support my work at the link in my bio." |

One per post. Rotate them by pillar.

## Feed vs Stories

- Feed: posts that can travel to non-followers.
- Stories: updates, thank-yous, behind the scenes, polls, links, daily proof. Roll up repeated updates into one weekly carousel.

## Repurposing

- Never re-upload the same file. Recut: new hook, new first frame, new length, new angle, or a reply to a comment.
- Turn a winning video into a carousel (and the reverse).
- Cross-post only original files without another platform's watermark.

## Content pillars

Define each pillar by the signal it earns:
- **Send pillar:** relatable truths, strong takes, "this is so you" moments.
- **Save pillar:** frameworks, checklists, how-tos, resources.
- **Act pillar:** proof, case studies, offers, behind-the-scenes of the work.
- **Talk pillar:** questions and opinions the audience has real stakes in.

Most accounts need one Send, one Save and one Act pillar at minimum.
