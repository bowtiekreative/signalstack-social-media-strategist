# Experiments

Social data is noisy. A single post can do 100 times better than the next for no reason anyone can name. Test so that noise cannot fool you.

## Rules

1. **One variable.** Change the hook, or the cover, or the length. Not all three.
2. **Same idea.** Compare two versions of one idea, not topic A vs topic B. Topic tests need many dozens of posts per side before a real difference shows.
3. **Per-reach metric, decided in advance.** Usually send rate or hold. Write it down before posting.
4. **Pairs, not singles.** Run at least 5 pairs before drawing a conclusion. Compare within each pair, then count how many pairs the variant won.
5. **Stop rule.** Decide the number of pairs up front. Do not stop the moment one side looks good.
6. **Big effects only.** With typical volumes you can detect large differences (2x), not small ones (10%). Say this to the user.
7. **Log it.** Date, idea, variable, version, metric, result, decision.

## Reading the result

- Variant wins 5 of 5 pairs: adopt.
- 4 of 5: likely better; adopt and keep watching.
- 3 of 5 or less: no evidence of a difference; pick by preference or cost.

## Good first tests

| Test | Variable | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Hook styles | contradiction vs specific number | hold, send rate |
| Covers | face vs text-only | carousel swipe-through, reach |
| Length | 20 s vs 45 s | full watch-through |
| Call to action | send ask vs save ask | send rate vs save rate |
| Feed vs Stories for updates | placement | feed reach of other posts that week |
