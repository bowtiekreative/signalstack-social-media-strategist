## Three of your last 90 posts did half your reach. Do you know why?

Neither does anyone you've paid.

You've done what the gurus said. Post every day. Use the trending audio. Add 30 hashtags. Ask people to "comment YES."

And the numbers still look like a coin flip.

Here's what nobody tells you: **platforms don't rank your post on likes.** They rank it on what they predict each viewer will do next. Stop. Watch. Send it to a friend. Save it. Follow you. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn have all published this. Almost nobody builds their content strategy around it.

**SignalStack does.** It turns Claude into a senior social media strategist who reads your numbers per person reached, finds the leak, and hands you week one, written.

## What SignalStack does for you

Drop in your analytics export (or connect your scheduler) and ask one question.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔍 **Audits** | Breaks every post down per person reached, finds the handful of posts carrying your account, and flags the habits quietly costing you reach. |
| 🩺 **Diagnoses** | Names the exact layer that's leaking, with the number that proves it. No vibes. |
| 🎯 **Prioritizes** | Ranks fixes into *Fix first*, *Do next* and *Test*, with honest low-to-high ranges, not hype. |
| 🗓️ **Plans** | Builds content pillars, format mix, cadence, a conversion path and a 30-day content calendar. |
| ✍️ **Creates** | Writes the scroll-stopping hooks, short-form video scripts, carousels and captions. Ready to post. |
| 🧪 **Tests** | Designs A/B experiments that noise can't fool, plus a weekly scorecard. |

## The Signal Stack: the six layers every post has to climb

Find the layer that's leaking. Fix it first. Everything above it starts working.

| Layer | The viewer... | You measure |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Stop** | stops scrolling | 3-second views, skip rate |
| **2. Hold** | stays | watch time, % watched, dwell |
| **3. Send** | sends it to someone | shares per reach |
| **4. Save** | keeps it | saves per reach |
| **5. Talk** | starts a real conversation | comments per reach |
| **6. Act** | moves toward you | follows, profile visits, link taps, sales |

**Most accounts obsess over layer 5 and starve layer 3.** Sends are how a post reaches people who don't follow you yet. SignalStack shows you which one you're starving.

## Install in 60 seconds

### Claude apps (web, desktop, mobile)
1. Download **`social-media-strategist.zip`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/bowtiekreative/signalstack-social-media-strategist/releases/latest).
2. In Claude, go to **Customize > Skills**, click **+**, then **Create skill > Upload a skill**, and upload the ZIP.
3. Turn on **Code execution and file creation** in **Settings > Capabilities** if it isn't already.

### Claude Code
```bash
git clone https://github.com/bowtiekreative/signalstack-social-media-strategist ~/.claude/skills/social-media-strategist
```
Skills uploaded in the Claude apps also sync to Claude Code when you sign in with the same account.

**That's it.** Just talk about your social media. SignalStack switches on by itself.

## FAQ

**Is SignalStack free?**
Yes. MIT licensed, free forever. If it earns its keep, [buy me a coffee](https://support.ryanperez.ca).

**Which platforms does it support?**
Instagram (Reels, carousels, feed, Stories), TikTok, YouTube (Shorts and long-form), LinkedIn and Facebook.

**Do I need an analytics export?**
No, but it helps. It works from native exports (Meta Business Suite, TikTok Studio, YouTube Studio, LinkedIn analytics), scheduler data (Publer, Buffer, Later, Metricool, Sprout) or even screenshots. With no data at all it still builds a strategy and writes content, and tells you what to start measuring.

**Will it make me go viral?**
Nobody honest can promise that. SignalStack finds where your reach is leaking, gives you a realistic range, and writes the content to fix it.

**Does it work with ChatGPT or other AI tools?**
It's built as a Claude skill. The reference files are plain markdown, so you can read and borrow the playbook anywhere.
